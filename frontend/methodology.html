How the screening works

Data connection review: 9 October 2026

These free tools help you identify data gaps, prepare evidence and decide what to investigate next. Every result applies to the geometry and information submitted.

EUDR plot check

  1. We validate the uploaded geometry and coordinate format.
  2. We read the JRC Global Forest Cover 2020 map (V4, 10 m) for the historical forest baseline.
  3. We read Hansen Global Forest Change 2025 v1.13 (approximately 30 m) for tree-cover loss during 2021–2025, counting pixels inside the plot.
  4. RADD radar alerts supplement these reads when the radar connection is available. An unavailable radar connection is recorded as unavailable.

Loss detected means a loss signal needs investigation. Clear in this screen means the required historical map reads succeeded without a post-2020 loss signal in the covered years. Inconclusive means the required baseline, coverage or data read could not support a conclusion.

Satellite tree-cover loss is a signal, not a legal determination of deforestation. Plantation harvest, land use, legality, traceability and the remaining due-diligence evidence require separate assessment. Annual Hansen data currently ends in 2025; a result does not establish absence of clearing in 2026 or provide EUDR certification or an official Due Diligence Statement.

Carbon screening

The tool screens HTI and HA scenarios for indicative REDD and improved forest management opportunities. Peat scenarios and missing essential evidence receive a review flag. Other permit types require manual assessment.

Hansen supplies annual loss through 2025 and historical year-2000 canopy. Historical canopy is an input to the forest screen; it does not establish present forest condition by itself. Loss estimates use the latest eight available annual observations. Biomass comes from the configured satellite source when available, otherwise a clearly labelled IPCC Tier 1 default.

The reported range uses a fixed 30-year illustrative scenario. Permit years remaining are checked separately. Thirty years is a screening assumption, not an approved crediting period or a promise of issued credits. Project-specific rights, baseline, additionality, leakage, permanence and the applicable standard need specialist confirmation.

Missing essential satellite loss or canopy data produces a review result with no carbon quantity. A usable range remains an initial scenario for further investigation, rather than a verified inventory, registry approval, financial valuation or forecast of saleable credits.

Coverage and data gaps

Small plots below pixel resolution, plots crossing a Hansen tile boundary and unusually large raster windows may require a smaller polygon or manual review. Only the submitted polygon is assessed; neighbouring land is excluded. Test fixtures are disabled in production.
