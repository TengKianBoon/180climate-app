# Fieldwork open public-beta boundary

**Decision date:** 11 September 2026  
**Status:** implemented locally; publication checkpoint pending  
**Service:** `fieldwork.match_intro.v1`

## Approved operating model

The public front door and operating criteria are open. Any requester or service provider may register without an invitation code. Personal profiles, requests, contact details and precise sites are not public or searchable.

180Climate:

- receives a request or capability registration;
- reviews scope, authority, safety, geography, availability, language and declared capability;
- may request clarification or decline an unsafe, unlawful, unauthorised or out-of-scope request;
- shows each named party the fields proposed for disclosure; and
- facilitates an introduction only after both parties consent and an operator confirms the introduction.

The requester and provider independently decide whether to proceed and directly agree scope, access, safety, contract, price, tax, payment, permits, insurance, confidentiality and disputes.

## Service role exclusions

The public beta does not make 180Climate the employer, employment agency, worker dispatcher, supervisor, underlying service contractor, payment custodian, credential certifier or guarantor. A registration or potential match is not a booking, access permission, professional certification, endorsement, contract or guarantee.

No public profile directory, marketplace search, bidding, platform messaging, scheduling, contract execution, payment processing, automated dispatch or regulated conclusion is included.

## Data boundary

Public:

- service description, categories, review criteria and exclusions;
- open-registration process and price status;
- aggregate synthetic test evidence; and
- versioned source, schemas and documentation.

Non-public:

- names and contact details;
- requester/provider submissions and operator notes;
- precise sites, credentials and identity documents;
- disclosure previews, consents and introductions; and
- runtime database, access keys, backups and operational logs.
