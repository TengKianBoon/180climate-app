# ADR-0004 — Lead store

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-06-21 · **Deciders:** John (owner), Claude (architect)

## Context
Every lead (user who submits the form) must be captured durably and delivered to 180Climate for follow-up.

## Decision
**Email to `info@180climate.net`** (primary) **+ Google Sheet append** (durable backup). App runtime uses its own credentials, not Claude's connectors. Credentials live in host environment config — **never in the repo**.

On detailed-report download: email `info@180climate.net` with the DOCX attached + the full lead-form details, subject `"{concession name} — {filename}"`, and append the lead to the Google Sheet.

## Consequences
+ Simple, reliable, no CRM dependency for v1.
+ Google Sheet gives John a queryable list without a database.
− Email-only primary = no real-time dashboard; acceptable for v1 lead volumes.
**Gate P** verifies this end-to-end (email received + attachment + Sheet row) before go-live.
