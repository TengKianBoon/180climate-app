- **Fix:**
  1. `core/data/cache.py`: add `encoding="utf-8"` to **both** `read_text` (L47) and `write_text` (L49).
  2. Re-encode the 7 fixtures cp1252→UTF-8 (read as cp1252, write as utf-8 — the byte becomes a proper UTF-8 em-dash).
