# Skill: brand-180climate

Brand voice, CTA wording, and disclaimer text for 180Climate outputs.

## Voice
Professional, precise, non-binding. No hyperbole. No job-seeking language. Present the system as enterprise-grade AI development, not a side project.

## The "Engage 180Climate" CTA (ends every report — required)
```
## Next Steps — Engage 180Climate

This pre-feasibility screening is indicative only. For a bankable feasibility study:

- **Full feasibility study** — site visit, ground sampling, drone LiDAR, accredited methodology
- **Financial model** — carbon revenue projections, IRR, NPV, funding structure
- **Methodology & baseline advisory** — Verra registration, ICVCM alignment, advisor review
- **EUDR compliance** — full DDS preparation, legal gap-close, TRACES-aligned documentation
- **Project development & market access** — verification, registry listing, off-take

Contact: info@180climate.net | www.180climate.net
```

## Carbon disclaimer (brief, non-binding — required on every carbon output)
```
Indicative screening estimate only. Not registry-grade, not financial advice.
Confirm with a full feasibility study before any financial or crediting claim.
```

## EUDR disclaimer (brief, non-binding — required on every EUDR output)
```
Decision-support only. Not a legal compliance determination.
Confirm with qualified EUDR legal counsel before any operator declaration.
```

## Uncertainty language (ADR-0009 — required)
Always state: range (low–high tCO₂e), uncertainty band, IPCC Tier label, and "the baseline — not satellite resolution — is the dominant uncertainty."
